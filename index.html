08 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
worth knowing.

These answers guide visitors towards the information they need. Confirm current arrangements with the operator.

How do I reserve a table?+

Follow the reservation link on the official BusCuisine website. This concept does not process bookings or confirm availability.

How long does the journey take?+

The public homepage describes a dinner journey of approximately 2.5 hours. Confirm the duration for your selected experience.

Can I request a specific seat?+

Submit seating preferences through the official booking process or confirm them directly with the operator. Preferences may not be guaranteed.

Can dietary requirements be accommodated?+

The public homepage says dietary requirements and allergies can be accommodated with advance notice. Confirm your specific needs directly before reserving.

Where does the journey depart from?+

The public site lists Protea Hotel Marriott, North Wharf, Foreshore, Cape Town as a location. Verify the current departure point and arrival instructions before travelling.

Are accessibility arrangements available?+

Contact BusCuisine directly to discuss mobility requirements and confirm suitability before booking.

Can I change a reservation or bring children?+

Change policies and child-related arrangements must be confirmed through the official booking channel.
